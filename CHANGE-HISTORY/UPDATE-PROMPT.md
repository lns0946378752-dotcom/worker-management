# Prompt cap nhat lich su thay doi

Dung prompt nay tren may hien tai sau moi dot lam viec co thay doi trong repository:

> Hay xem thay doi thuc te trong Git va cap nhat lich su tai folder `CHANGE-HISTORY/` cua repository nay. Folder nay chi duoc chua ba file: `major-changes.md`, `minor-changes.md`, va `UPDATE-PROMPT.md`; khong tao them file khac trong folder.
>
> - Thay doi lon (kien truc, cong nghe chinh, pham vi san pham/chuc nang, quy trinh chay hoac cau truc repo) ghi vao `major-changes.md`.
> - Thay doi nho (bug fix nho, dieu chinh giao dien/tai lieu/config, hoac kiem tra) ghi vao `minor-changes.md`.
> - Cap nhat file phu hop trong `PROJECT-TRACKING/`: anh huong toan he thong ghi o `system-impact.md`; anh huong mot/vai chuc nang ghi o `feature-impact.md`. Them lien ket tu README neu tao huong dan quan trong moi.
> - Ghi prompt nguoi dung thuc te va noi dung/quyet dinh phan hoi vao `PROJECT-TRACKING/PROMPT-AND-RESPONSE-LOG.md`. Khong bia prompt qua khu; ghi ro neu phan lich su cu chi la tom luoc.
> - Moi muc ghi ro: ngay gio kem mui gio, ten may cap nhat, repository/branch va commit lien quan neu co, tom tat thay doi, file/pham vi lien quan, ket qua kiem tra, va trang thai con han che.
> - Dung ten may tu he thong hien tai; lay thoi gian hien tai kem mui gio he thong. Neu khong xac dinh duoc thong tin nao, ghi “khong xac dinh”, khong tu doan.
> - Phan biet thay doi vua lam tren may nay voi thay doi chi duoc pull/merge tu remote. Khong ghi cong cua may nay cho commit co san tu remote.
> - Khong ghi mat khau, token, noi dung `.env`, hay du lieu ca nhan. Neu khong co thay doi Git, co the ghi ket qua kiem tra vao file nho nhung phai noi ro khong co thay doi ma nguon.
> - Doc cac ghi chep hien co de noi tiep dung format; khong xoa/sua lich su cu, khong tao commit neu chua duoc yeu cau.
> - Ket thuc bang viec kiem tra `git status` va xac nhan folder `CHANGE-HISTORY/` chi co dung ba file tren.
